Get involved

Three ways to help Unako open its doors.

Unako is in its founding stage — every rand, hour and connection right now goes directly toward opening the centre sooner.

Donate

Funds go directly against the build budget — from a borehole to a first counsellor's salary. Every amount moves us closer to opening day.

Contact us to donate

Volunteer

We need mentors, workshop facilitators, and people willing to travel to outlying villages to teach what GBV is and how to respond to it.

Offer your time

Partner

Schools, corporates and health providers can co-run workshops, sponsor equipment, or host team-building days once the centre opens.

Start a conversation
