What we offer

Support that meets people where they are.

Every service is designed to be walked into without shame — whether the visit is about a relationship, a habit, or a job application.

GBV response and counselling

A confidential first point of contact for anyone experiencing or leaving an abusive relationship, with guidance on reporting and next steps.

Family and shelter support

Working with local government toward emergency shelter for those who need to get away from an abuser, plus support for the family left navigating the fallout.

Mental health support

Space to talk through trauma, anxiety and the everyday weight of rural life, with mentors and, over time, professional counsellors.

Drug and alcohol support

Honest, non-judgemental support for young people and adults dealing with addiction, and the family members affected by it.

Employment workshops

CV writing, interview practice and links to local opportunities, so a fresh start isn't blocked by not knowing where to begin.

Sexual and reproductive health

Clear, stigma-free information and referrals on sexual health, delivered by people trained to talk to young people honestly.

Healthy relationships

Workshops on what a respectful, safe partnership looks like — aimed at catching the warning signs long before they escalate.
